# Short+


## The Concept:
Short+ is a concept prototype exploring short-form video as a discovery page for deeper content.

Instead of keeping users within an endless short-video feed, the experience is designed to guide users from short-form discovery into longer, deeper content — including full videos, stories, episodes, and related topics.


## Key Features:
- Short+ / Long+ — Switch between short-form discovery and deeper content.
- Swipe to Learn More — Move naturally from a short video into related content.
- Related Content — Discover full videos, stories, episodes, and related topics.
- Vertical Video Feed — Browse and discover content through a mobile-first short-video experience.
- Responsive Presentation — Designed for both mobile and desktop viewing.
